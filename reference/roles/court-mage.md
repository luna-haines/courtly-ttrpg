# Court Mage
You are a mage of the court, funded by the king/queen to pursue your magical research. You must, of course, help the court with it's magical needs from time to time, but to you it's worth it for the library, the workspace, and the resources you couldn't get anywhere else.

## Benefits
Whenever you draw a 9 or 10, you may set that card aside and draw another card instead (you must use the new card's value for the draw). If you do, whenever you would draw a card you may instead draw the card you set aside (put it in your discard pile after doing this).
